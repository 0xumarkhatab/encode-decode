AES-256-CBC

Encrypt and decrypt in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the page forgets everything when you refresh or close it.

How it works

You give a secret and an input. The page gives the AES-256-CBC result as base64. The result matches encode-decode.com.

The page deletes all data on every refresh. It also deletes all data when you close the page or press Clear all. Nothing is saved or sent.

The same input and secret always give the same output. This is good for compatibility, but do not use it to protect real secrets.
