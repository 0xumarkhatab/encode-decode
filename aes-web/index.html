AES-256-CBC

Encrypt and decrypt in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the page forgets everything when you refresh or close it.

How it works

This matches encode-decode.com. The key is your secret as UTF-8 bytes, padded with zeros to 32 bytes (longer secrets are cut at 32). The IV is 16 zero bytes, padding is PKCS#7, and the output is base64.

Because the IV never changes, the same input and secret always give the same output. That’s fine for compatibility, but don’t rely on it to protect real secrets.
